Fine-Tuning Results
Experimental Overview
The Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct model was fine-tuned for financial sentiment
classification using the Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) method. The
objective was to adapt the pretrained language model to classify
financial headlines into Positive, Neutral, and Negative sentiment
categories.

The fine-tuning process completed successfully over three epochs,
comprising 384 optimization steps.
Training and Evaluation Behavior
The training loss decreased substantially during the initial stages of
optimization and remained low as training progressed. The final training
loss was 0.0674.

The evaluation loss also decreased during the early stages of training,
reaching an approximate minimum of 0.20 around optimization step
234. During the later stages, it increased slightly and finished at
0.2165.

The difference between the final training and evaluation losses is
0.1491. The lower training loss compared with the evaluation loss,
together with the slight rise in evaluation loss near the end of
training, suggests the possibility of mild overfitting. However, the
observed curves do not by themselves establish severe overfitting.
Generalization should be assessed further using independent test data
and classification metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and
Macro-F1.
